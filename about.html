About Marygold

A school in Elelenwo, Port Harcourt, since 2002.

Our story

Marygold International School was founded in 2002 and welcomes boys and girls from crèche to secondary, as day pupils or boarders.

Our motto, Solid Foundation, is the idea behind everything we teach. Get the basics right early, and children carry them for life.

You will find us at 58 Station Road, Elelenwo, in Port Harcourt.

What we stand for

Strong basics

Reading, writing and numbers come first, because everything else is built on them.

The whole child

Science, music, ICT (information and communication technology), and practical skills sit beside the classroom subjects.

Working with parents

We want families to know how their child is doing and to feel free to ask.
